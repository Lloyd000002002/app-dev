# The Gorge

An American science fiction romantic action horror film directed by Scott Derrickson and written by Zach Dean.

---

## Synopsis
Two highly trained elite operatives, **Levi** and **Drasa**, are assigned to separate guard towers standing on opposite sides of a classified, massive gorge. Ordered never to communicate, they grow close from a distance while defending against a hidden, cataclysmic evil emerging from deep below.

> "Two operatives guard opposite sides of a classified gorge, bonding at a distance while defending against a hidden evil."

## Key Achievements and Impact
* **Genre Blending:** Highly unique concept that blends romance, science fiction, action, and horror into one story.
* **Launch Milestone:** Released globally as a major streaming title, securing a highly enthusiastic reception from action and thriller fans.
* **Creative Soundtrack:** Features a distinct, atmospheric musical score composed by Trent Reznor and Atticus Ross.
